## 🔬 1. Research Projects

### 1.1 Computational Investigation of TET2 Variants Associated with Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML)
* **Folder:** [`TET2-AML/`](./TET2-AML)
* **Research Area:** Cancer Biology | Molecular Biology | Structural Bioinformatics
* **Overview:** Evolutionary conservation, protein stability, structural modeling, and protein–ligand interaction analyses of conserved C1263 missense substitutions in the catalytic domain of Ten-Eleven Translocation 2 (TET2) (PDB: 4NM6) associated with AML.
* **Key Tools:** PyMOL, MEGA12, GEPIA2, DisGeNET, Open Targets, STRING, Cytoscape, I-Mutant, ProtParam, PROCHECK/SAVES, CB-Dock2, Discovery Studio.
* **Status:** Manuscript preparation completed.

---

### 1.2 Molecular Detection and Quantitative Analysis of BCR-ABL1 in Chronic Myeloid Leukemia (CML)
* **Folder:** [`BCR-ABL/`](./BCR-ABL)
* **Research Area:** Molecular Oncology | Hematological Malignancies | Molecular Diagnostics
* **Overview:** Quantitative evaluation of *BCR-ABL1* transcript variants (Major, Minor, and Micro), *BCR-ABL1/ABL1* ratios, International Scale (IS) values, and longitudinal molecular response monitoring in Bangladeshi CML patients.
* **Collaboration:** Conducted under the supervision of **Dr. Md. Morshedul Alam** (Associate Professor, Dept. of Biochemistry & Microbiology, North South University) in clinical collaboration with **Dr. Monir** (Labaid).
* **Assay Platforms:** TRUPCR® BCR-ABL1 Kit, geneMAP™ BCR-ABL1 p210 IS-MMR Detection Kit.
* **Status:** Initial data analysis completed; manuscript development in progress.
