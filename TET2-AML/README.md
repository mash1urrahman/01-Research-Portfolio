# Computational Investigation of TET2 Variants Associated with Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML)

## Project Overview

This research project investigates conserved missense substitutions in the catalytic domain of Ten-Eleven Translocation 2 (TET2), a key enzyme involved in DNA demethylation.

The study combines evolutionary, structural, and protein–ligand interaction analyses to explore the potential functional consequences of selected TET2 variants associated with acute myeloid leukemia (AML).

## Research Focus

- TET2 and DNA demethylation
- Conserved missense substitutions
- Evolutionary conservation analysis
- Protein structural analysis
- Protein stability assessment
- Protein–ligand interactions
- Molecular implications in AML

## Research Objectives

1. Investigate selected missense substitutions in the TET2 catalytic domain.
2. Evaluate evolutionary conservation across selected species.
3. Explore potential structural and stability alterations.
4. Examine protein–ligand interactions involving relevant catalytic components.

## Computational Workflow

The project involves:

- Sequence and variant investigation
- Evolutionary conservation analysis
- Protein structure visualization
- Protein stability assessment
- Protein–ligand interaction analysis
- Biological interpretation of computational findings
